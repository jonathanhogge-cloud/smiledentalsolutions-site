Why sellers choose us

You've built something. Choose who carries it forward.

Most buyers calling Indiana dentists today are backed by private equity, often without saying so. Their model depends on reselling the platform in a few years. Ours is designed so the practice ends up owned by the dentists and staff who run it.

Transaction options

Structured around where you are in your career

Every practice and every owner is different. These are the structures we use most often, and they can be combined.

Full sale

Sell and step back on your schedule

We buy the practice outright. You can stay on as an associate for an agreed transition period or hand off sooner.

  • Valued on trailing collections
  • Transition period that fits you
  • Staff and patients stay in place
Partnership

Take cash now and keep a stake

Part of the price is paid at closing and part is kept as equity in the platform, so you share in what the group becomes.

  • Cash at closing plus retained equity
  • Keep practicing as long as you want
  • Equity exits through employee ownership
Retirement transition

Plan a handoff over the next few years

For owners looking 1–3 years out. We can phase the transition, add an associate, and use seller financing where it helps both sides.

  • Phased timeline
  • Seller-financing options
  • Associate recruiting support
Real estate

Own your building? We'll work with it

We can buy the property, lease it from you, or finance it through you. Rent stays at market, and your building keeps earning for you.

  • Purchase, lease, or seller-financed
  • Market-rate long-term leases
  • Handled alongside the practice deal

Employee ownership

The exit goes to your people, not to another buyer

Our long-term structure is an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP). Instead of the group being sold to a larger fund, ownership moves over time to the dentists, hygienists, assistants and front desk staff who keep the practices running. For sellers who keep equity, that is how the equity turns into cash.

  1. You sell or partnerCash at closing, with optional retained equity in the platform.
  2. The practice joins the groupShared credentialing, purchasing, payroll and compliance. Your clinical style stays yours.
  3. The group growsMore Indiana practices join under the same doctor-led model.
  4. Ownership moves to employeesThe ESOP buys out retained equity, and your team becomes owners.

The process

From first call to closing in about four months

Everything stays confidential. Your staff and patients hear about it when you decide they should.

  1. Week 1

    Conversation

    A private call or visit about your goals, timeline and practice.

  2. Weeks 2–4

    Practice review

    Under an NDA, we review production, collections and payer mix.

  3. Week 5

    Letter of intent

    A written offer with price, structure, real estate and transition terms.

  4. 45 days

    Due diligence

    One organized document request, not a stream of follow-ups.

  5. 60 days

    Closing

    Purchase agreement, lease or real estate, and funding.

  6. After

    Transition

    Credentialing, payroll and systems move over without disrupting patients.
